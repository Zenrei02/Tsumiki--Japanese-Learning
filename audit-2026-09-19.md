### ① `check-module-drift.py` now compares the lesson-completion block — and can fail

`LessonComplete()` and `COMPLETE_CSS` are compared across the root modules, with a new
`line_block()` extractor and an `expect_at_least=3` guard on `compare()`.

**Why a new extractor was needed, and why this is the real finding.** `fn_body()` would have
"worked" and been worthless: `function LessonComplete({ mark = "済", … }) {` opens its first
brace on the **destructured parameter list**, so a brace matcher closes on the end of the
parameters and returns a **107-character signature** — identical in every module by
construction. It would have printed a green ✅ forever while the 2,700-byte body drifted
underneath. `COMPLETE_CSS` is worse: a template literal full of `${…}`, which sends a brace
matcher off the end of the file. *(I hit both of these in the audit itself — my first
by-hand comparison returned 107 bytes and I nearly reported six identical copies on the
strength of it.)*

`expect_at_least` exists for the same reason: without it, a block that gets renamed reports
*"nothing to compare"* and the run still exits 0.

**Four controls, run before any pass was believed** — the standing rule, and the point of the
whole item:
