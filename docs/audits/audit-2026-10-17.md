I deleted the line-189 entry. The surviving entry's story now reads:

> Four strokes of heartbeat. You have been writing it for weeks: whole under 思 and 意,
> gone-from under 忘, squashed to 忄 at the left of 忙. Standing alone, it is the feeling all of
> them share — and 心配 finally reads: the heart distributed, worry. 安心 settles it: relief.

**Why this wording.** 心's card appears in **two** groups. In *Out and about*, `sb-kokoro` and
`cp-machi` show it, and line 189 was written for that place. In *The inner ledger*, `k-t38` and

so the story cannot say "now" or "at last". The new text keeps the first story's image (squashed

carries 忄, not 心, and 忙 is not one of `sb-kokoro`'s four characters. The phrase "gone-from
under 忘" is `sb-kokoro`'s own wording. The new text drops the lesson id `sb-kokoro`, which the
old line showed to learners verbatim (see ⑤).

**This is AI-authored Japanese teaching content. It goes to the reviewer.** It is queued as **Batch Y.1** in
`docs/reviewer/reviewer-content-batches-v1.md`, not treated as settled.
