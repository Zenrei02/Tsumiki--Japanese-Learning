# Component shape policy — the 57-shape pass

*Session 18, Aug 20 2026. Lloyd's ruling: this pass goes before Group 7. It
classifies every component shape Groups 3–5 introduced without a ruling (the 57
in `kanji-order-report-v3.md`), plus 氵 — taught in 洗 since Group 5, absent
from that list, and the shape that blocks 落・漢・菜 — plus 礻, which arrives
with Group 7's 社.*

## The rule being applied

A shape is **NAMED** when naming it pays for itself: it recurs in at least two
taught characters, or it stands in the demand-driven path of the next two
groups, or it is itself a character the syllabus will plausibly teach (the
者-in-暑 move: teaching the character later retro-names the part). A named
shape gets a short English gloss on the card and may carry a lesson mention.

A shape is **MUTED** when it appears once and buys nothing forward: the
character is taught whole and the decomposition stays hidden. Muting is not a
verdict on the etymology — it is a statement that the learner's attention is
better spent elsewhere. A muted shape can be promoted later the way 者 is being
promoted now; nothing is lost by starting muted.

Glosses are teaching judgements, AI-authored, and land on the existing
reviewer row for component glosses (the one carrying "legs" and "rice").

## What this unblocks

落・漢・菜 stop being blocked: 氵 and 艹 now have names. 漢 enters Group 7
(k-t29), completing 漢字 — the word this app says most often. 落 and 菜 wait
for Group 8, where 各 (落's other half) and 野 (菜's word-mate) earn their
places — admitting them now would cost two more unnamed parts, which is the
exact situation this pass exists to end.

Nothing in this document edits a module. The classifications take effect as
lessons are authored against them, starting with Group 7 in this session.
