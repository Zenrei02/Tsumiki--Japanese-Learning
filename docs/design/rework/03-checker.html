WHAT WE FOUND
FIX 直す
です、でも → 行くつもりですが、

Two sentences joined with a comma and でも reads as a run-on. Inside one sentence, join with が.

CORRECT, BUT UNNATURAL
お金があまりないです → お金があまりありません

Nothing wrong — but ないです sounds spoken. In writing, ありません is what a native would reach for.

WORTH KNOWING

つもり states a firm plan. If it is more of a hope, 〜ようと思っています softens it.
