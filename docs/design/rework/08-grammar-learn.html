Making a request · 〜てください Stage 1 · 日本語能力試験 N5 as reference
WHAT IT DOES

Take the て-form of a verb and add ください, and you have a polite request. It is the everyday way to ask someone to do something — a shop, a colleague, a friend's mother.

窓まどを開あけてください。
Please open the window.
ここに名前なまえを書かいてください。
Please write your name here.
WATCH OUT

ください on its own asks for a thing (水をください). To ask for an action, the て-form has to come first.
