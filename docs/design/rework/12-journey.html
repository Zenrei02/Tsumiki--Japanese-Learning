WHAT THIS BOARD IS FOR

Judging the feel of the tower, the build, and the map with the real materials before any app code exists. Drive it: tap blocks, try the filters, switch presets, and build the torii on About three months.

DECIDED — DRAWN AS GIVEN

  • A block is effort, never grading. No verdict colors, no corrections. A checker block shows its sentence exactly as written.
  • Wood with a section mark. The mark is a small seal in the section accent carrying the glyph Home already uses: あ ア 漢 文 言 直.
  • Login blocks are plain wood with no mark, and they settle into the foundation because they're the earliest blocks.
  • Showing up: one block a day, up to three a week (Lloyd, Oct 6 — was one a week). Builds come sooner. The checker stays at one a week.
  • The tower is all-time and never shrinks. Building lifts copies of blocks. The originals stay where they were.
  • No counts, no targets, no previews, and no unlit map points. The structure is announced in the past tense, once it has been earned.

MY CALLS — YOURS TO OVERTURN

  • Keystone: darker keyaki wood with a gold band across the top. There's no glyph, so it adds no new Japanese, and it's told apart by shape as well as tone. All the keystones are laid together, so a stage reads as one band across the tower.
  • Review blocks: the kit's 復 mark, in the accent of the section reviewed. I didn't use the kit's 藍 #3D5A80 for 復 because that is the checker's UNNATURAL color, and this board carries no verdict colors.
  • Filters: a section chip lights both that section's lessons and its reviews. "Reviews" lights every review block. Only one chip is active at a time. Tap it again to clear.
  • Tower shape: rows alternate a full course and a course one block shorter (running bond), and the tower narrows every few courses. The base grows with the pile. Blocks are laid oldest-first, so the foundation is early kana and showing up.
  • Castle site: Matsumoto, not Himeji. At inset size, Himeji sits on top of Hōryū-ji.

OPEN — LLOYD'S CALL

  • All English copy is a DRAFT.
  • Tower block size. In a year's tower the blocks are about 28px wide, which is under the 44px touch target. Options are a larger tap zone, pinch-to-zoom, or a tap that selects the nearest block.
  • Hiragana contrast. The あ seal uses the current #9C8BC4, which is 2.88:1 under its cream glyph. That's the same open fix as Home (ACCENT_RATIONALE). At this size it matters more.
  • The checker seal is 朱 #C7351B. It sits beside a learner's sentence in the detail sheet, where 朱 elsewhere means FIX. Consider whether that sheet should show the seal at all.
  • Okinawa and the outlying islands aren't drawn at this scale. An inset box is the usual answer if a site is ever placed there.
  • Place names in English ("Itsukushima Shrine") are proper nouns in Latin letters. Decide whether they count as romaji under the standing rule.
