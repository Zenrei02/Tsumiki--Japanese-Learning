## Ground rules (unchanged from the brief — restate them to Code every session)

- Whole-file replacements, never patches.
- `tsumiki-app/src/App.jsx` and `src/lib/tokens.js` are **generated by `build-vite-app.py`**. Change the root `.jsx` modules and the generator, then regenerate. Never hand-edit generated files.
- No lesson counts anywhere. The kanji coverage percentage is the one permitted number.
- The three tier colours are untouchable: 朱 `#C7351B` FIX, 藍 `#3D5A80` CORRECT-BUT-UNNATURAL, 金 `#907119` WORTH KNOWING; 緑 `#3E7C4F` stays "ok".
- Wordmark is lowercase `tsumiki`; chrome glyph is つ; Checker keeps 直し; Japanese-facing copy says 日本語能力試験, never "JLPT".
- No kanji inside the hiragana/katakana modules.
- All text sizes in `rem`. The boards use px at 16px = 1rem; divide by 16.
- Mobile first: 390px is the design width. Touch targets ≥ 44px.
- Netlify: one **deploying** push per phase. Iterate on `npm run dev`, which is free. Run the deploy check from `CLAUDE.md` before every push and report the result.

## The system in one paragraph

Tatami weave is the ground of every screen. Every sheet of content is washi. The things you press are wood (secondary) or vermilion lacquer (primary). Buttons sit proud of the surface on a hard lip and press down into it on tap. Calm everywhere; on any screen exactly one thing is red, and it is the thing to do next. The only place that gets more energy than that is the lesson-done screen, where the Next button gains a pulsing gold ring.
