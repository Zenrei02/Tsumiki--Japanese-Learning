# Stage 11 — N2 opens. The three-module blueprint

*Session 18, Aug 20 2026. Lloyd: "Let's see what the next stage would look like.
Do all three modules at a time. We're now stepping into N2 territory." This is
the coordinated plan — grammar, vocabulary and kanji as one decision — sized
against what is actually taught, not against a published N2 list.*

## What "N2" means from where the app stands

The N3 stages already swallowed the easy half of N2: とたん・最中・次第・くせに・
ながらも・一方・わけ family・べき・に違いない・だらけ・がたい are all taught, and
Steps 30/36 took most of the compound particles. Checked against the 383 taught
points, the genuinely new N2 territory groups into six themes — and that is the
stage: **Stage 11 · Nuance, Steps 44–49**, about 50 lessons with the usual
skill builders, culture connections, compositions, checkpoint and rc10.

## The synchronization rule (the point of this document)

The proposed banks demand **66 untaught characters** — four kanji groups on
their own. That is precisely how the N3 gap formed: grammar authored freely,
kanji chased it later. So this time the constraint runs both ways:

**Bank words are chosen partly by what Groups 8–9 can teach, and Groups 8–9
are composed partly by what the banks will need.** One decision, two views.

## Vocabulary — banks, scenes, and two new verb forms

Eight bank words per step, N2 register, every one scene-able. The spine is the
learner's second year: the job interview, the contract, the paperwork window,
the rumor at the office. Proposed (≈45 new words):

- **44** 残業・締め切り・断る・命令・引き受ける・諦める・支払い・無理
- **45** 面接・入社式・卒業・契約・更新・引っ越す・乾杯・司会
- **46** 全国・年齢・性別・分野・期間・対象・幅広い・国籍
- **47** 評判・専門家・割引・満員・休業日・経験者・値上げ・行列
- **48** 犯人・真実・誤解・判断・印象・証言・うわさ・当たり前
- **49** 書類・申請・承認・提出・増加・減少・発表・期限

**Scenes wave 2** covers ~20 of these (the interview chair, the moving-day
truck, the queue that IS the restaurant's 評判). All-new JP lines — N2 grammar
in them — so the whole wave is reviewer-flagged, unlike wave 1's reuse trick.

**FORMS ladder additions** (the "N3 as triggered" mechanism, extended to N2):
ざる (行かざる — feeds ざるを得ない), まい, かねる, つつ. Each lands with its
grammar point id so the ladder test keeps passing; conjugator rules are
mechanical (nai-stem+ざる, stem+かねる/つつ, dict+まい with する→しまい).

## Kanji — Groups 8 and 9, composed against the banks

**Group 8 · Out and about (17)** — corpus demand leaders + the shape-pass
redemptions + the bank characters cheap to teach now:

> 出 早 店 読 方 少 家 心 持 忘 野 菜 落 切 受 引 別

Corpus demand: 出16 早21 店20 読17 方15 少15 忘14 家10 心10 持10. 落・菜 were
admitted by the 57-shape pass (各 gets named here); 野 rides 里+予, both ready;
心・少 redeem the pass's promissory notes; 切・受・引・別 are Stage 11 bank
characters (締め切り, 受付, 引き受ける／割引, 性別). Lessons would follow the
Group 7 shape: 4×4–5 chars + sb (心 as component family: 思・意・忘 reread) +
cp. "In the wild" lines per lesson, per the new standard.

**Group 9 · Paperwork (16, sketch)** — the Step 49 register: 申 請 類 提 実
判 断 期 限 全 対 面 約 験 経 員. Composed properly only after the Stage 11
banks are real and the ledger has been turned — listed here as direction, not
commitment.

**Coverage arithmetic:** 145 + 17 + 16 = 178 taught. With the banks authored,
grammar-content token coverage lands ≈ 74–78% — the 80% line comes into reach
at Group 10 instead of receding.

## Build order (dependencies, not preference)

1. **Lloyd rules** on the six steps, the banks, and Group 8's 17.
2. **Banks author first** → ledger turn → Group 8 confirmed against *real*
   demand, then authored with wilds.
3. **Vocab rebuild** — the new words get cards; readings derived (UniDic) for
   whatever Group 8-9 don't cover.
4. **Grammar steps** author with arcs; FORMS additions land with their point ids.
5. **Scenes wave 2**, then **Group 9** from the next ledger turn.

## The honest caveat

The reviewer just took on nine grading batches, and batches J–O of content
review are already queued. Stage 11 adds ≈50 lessons, ~45 words with readings,
~20 scenes, two kanji groups of stories and wilds — call it batches P–R. None
of it blocks the GO/TUNE verdict, but the unreviewed pile is compounding, and
Stage 11 built now is Stage 11 unverified for months. Building it anyway is a
defensible call — the reviewer works at human pace and the curriculum shouldn't
wait — but it is a call, and it is Lloyd's.
